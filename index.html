FAQ

Good questions.

What if my project does not fit a tier?

That is what the free scoping call is for. Niche, unusual and half-formed ideas are welcome. You leave the call with decisions made and get a fixed written quote within 48 hours.

Can you build for my specific OS or Linux distro?

Yes. Windows, macOS, every major Linux distribution (and plenty of niche ones), Android, iOS, the web, servers and Raspberry Pi. Packaging for your platform is part of the quote.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit books your slot, the rest is due on delivery. Projects over $10,000 are split into milestones, each with a demo.

Who owns the code?

You do. Repositories and accounts are created under your ownership or transferred at handover, with documentation and a walkthrough.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, happily — mention it when you book and it will be signed before any details are shared.

How quickly can you start?

Pick a time on the booking page: the calendar shows real availability. Small jobs (a Quick Win or a Launch site) often start the same week.
