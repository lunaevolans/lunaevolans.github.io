FAQ

Money questions.

Are these prices final?

Tier prices are fixed for the scope described. “From” prices mark tiers where scope varies; you get a fixed written quote before any work starts.

What if my project sits between two tiers?

Pick the closer one and mention it in the intake form, or book a free scoping call. Tiers are starting points, not boxes.

How do deposits work?

50% books your slot and the rest is due on delivery. Projects over $10,000 are split into milestones with a demo for each.

Do you offer refunds?

Deposits are refundable in full until work begins. After that, you only pay for milestones you have accepted. See the payment policy for details.
